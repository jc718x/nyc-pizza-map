Worth the Trip in Staten Island (3 spots)

Campania Coal-Fired Pizza $$ Neapolitan ⭐ Worth a special trip

A coal-fired Neapolitan pizzeria on Richmond Avenue in Staten Island, one of the borough's best destinations for a quality sit-down pie — worth a trip from anywhere on the island.
Joe & Pat's $ Classic Slice ⭐ Worth a special trip

A Staten Island institution since 1960, known for its cracker-thin crust with a secret Neapolitan recipe in the family since the founders came over from Naples.
Lee's Tavern $ Bar pie ⭐ Worth a special trip

A Dongan Hills corner bar with no sign and no website — you have to know to look for it. The thin, crispy bar pies are among the best on the island, and the cash-only, regulars-first vibe is entirely genuine.

About Pizza in Staten Island

Port Richmond is the anchor, and the island's pizzerias skew toward the sit-down, family-run end rather than the counter slice. Fewer places overall than the other boroughs, but a higher share of them are worth the ferry.
