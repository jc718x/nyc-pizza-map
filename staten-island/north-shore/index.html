About Pizza in North Shore

The North Shore is where Staten Island pizza is most concentrated and most argued-over. Port Richmond Avenue alone holds several of the borough's longest-running rooms, including the coal-and-thin-crust institutions people travel from Brooklyn and New Jersey to reach.

The house style here leans toward the bar pie — thin, crisp, cooked in a small round pan — and toward sit-down, family-run rooms rather than counter slices. St. George puts you within walking distance of the ferry terminal.
