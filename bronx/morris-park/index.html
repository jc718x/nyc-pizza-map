About Pizza in Morris Park

Morris Park is one of the last Bronx neighborhoods where the Italian-American presence is still the everyday character of the place rather than a heritage strip. The pizzerias reflect that: family-run rooms, long-standing counters, and a few sit-down restaurants that have fed the same blocks for decades.

Morris Park Avenue carries the bulk of it, with a second cluster a few blocks north on Williamsbridge Road. Unlike Arthur Avenue, this isn't a destination strip — it's a neighborhood that happens to have more good pizza per block than most of the borough.
