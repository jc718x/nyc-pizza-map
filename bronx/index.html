Worth the Trip in Bronx (1 spots)

Zero Otto Nove $$ Neapolitan ⭐ Worth a special trip

Named after the area code of owner Roberto Paciullo's hometown of Salerno, this Arthur Avenue trattoria is the most celebrated pizza destination in the Bronx. Wood-fired pies with a puffy Sorrento-style crust, sourcing mozzarella from the legendary Casa della Mozzarella down the block.
