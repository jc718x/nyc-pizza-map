Neighborhood Guide

Best Pizza in Bay Ridge

Walk down 3rd Avenue or 5th Avenue in Bay Ridge and within a few blocks you can stop into a neighborhood pizza place, grab a slice to go and keep moving — then duck into a sit-down spot a few doors down for a proper meal. That's Bay Ridge. It's got great restaurants across the board, and its pizza and Italian food scene is one of the strongest parts of it.

Bay Ridge has a mix of everything: classic NY slices, Sicilian, grandma, brick oven, Neapolitan, old-school Italian-American restaurant pies. That's actually one of its best appeals. The main pizza corridors run up and down 3rd, 4th, and 5th Avenue from around 75th Street down to 99th — a stretch that still has plenty of classic old-school neighborhood staples that have been around for decades. That history, combined with the neighborhood's long Italian-American roots and its strong restaurant culture, has made Bay Ridge a genuine destination for pizza in New York City.

The neighborhood is for everyone — locals grabbing slices, families, people making the trip from elsewhere. It's one of the most underrated pizza neighborhoods in the five boroughs, and one of the few that still feels rooted in the real thing.

All Bay Ridge Pizzerias (12 spots)

Frankie's of Bay Ridge $

A hidden gem off the corner of 3rd Avenue where you can watch them make the pizza from your seat. The kind of place Bay Ridge locals know and visitors almost never find.
Pizza Wagon $ ⭐ Worth a special trip

The pizzeria of Bay Ridge. Its sign says "A True Bay Ridginal," and it earns the title — right on 5th Avenue and 86th Street, the thoroughfare from the Verrazano Bridge into the neighborhood's shopping and nightlife district. Known for its squares. You can order and pay right from the sidewalk and keep moving.
Nino's $

A Bay Ridge institution doing old-school NY slices for decades.
Elegante Pizzeria $

A 5th Avenue slice shop with a steady, loyal following.
Espresso Pizzeria $

A 5th Avenue slice shop at the southern end of Bay Ridge, near Fort Hamilton.
Peppino's Pizza & Restaurant $

A full sit-down pizzeria on 3rd Avenue with a complete Italian-American menu.
Nonno's Pizza $

A 3rd Avenue neighborhood pizzeria named for the grandfather who started it.
Leo's Casa Calamari $

A 3rd Avenue institution known as much for its calamari as its pizza.
Brooklyn Firefly $$

A great spot for a pizza with a drink — and on a random weeknight you might find a live jazz band playing. Wood-fired, neighborhood-restaurant feel, exactly the kind of place Bay Ridge does better than most of Brooklyn.
APizza – Dyker Heights $$

A New Haven-style apizza outpost right on the Dyker Heights border, long-fermented dough.
Vesuvio Restaurant & Pizzeria $$

An Italian-American trattoria named after the volcano that defines Naples.
Mancini's Wood-Fired Pizza $$

A 5th Avenue wood-fired spot bringing proper Neapolitan-style pies to the neighborhood.
