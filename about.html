About this site

A love letter to New York pizza

I grew up in Bensonhurst, Brooklyn, in the heart of the Italian-American community. Pizza has always been more than food where I come from — it's the table everyone gathers around, it's the place you stopped at after school with your friends, it's the delivery guy that you knew on a first name basis, it's the thing you argue about with conviction and eat without ceremony. It's so much more than just the combination of sauce, cheese, and dough — to us, pizza was life.

How the list is made

This is a curated "best of," not a full directory. Every spot on the map has to earn its place. The list is built by aggregating critical consensus — sources like Eater, The Infatuation, One Bite, the New York Times, as well as my own personal taste.

Something wrong? Something missing?

Places close, pizzaiolos move on, and the city never stops changing. If you spot an error or know a spot that deserves to be here, use the Suggest a Spot link — I read every message.
